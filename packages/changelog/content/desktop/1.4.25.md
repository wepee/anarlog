---
date: "2026-09-24"
summary: "BlackMushi 1.4.25 fixes meeting export imports, lets each note pick its own transcription language, and stops titles from being cut off mid-word."
---

## Imports

- Import a meeting export folder that previously stopped at **Everything is
  already here** without bringing anything in
- Keep the provider's own summary in the summary document instead of pasting it
  into your memo, so the memo holds only what you wrote
- Attach the recording that came with an imported meeting, which was silently
  dropped before

## Transcription

- Re-transcribe a note in a specific language from the transcript menu or the
  note's overflow menu, with the current one marked
- The language you pick sticks to that note, so later recordings and
  re-transcriptions of it keep using it instead of falling back to your primary
  language

## Titles

- Stop saving a title that the model was cut off mid-word, in both note titles
  and chat titles. Reasoning models spent the whole budget thinking before
  writing the title, and the fragment was kept

## Audio and appearance

- Halve the size of exported recordings whose two channels carry the same
  signal, which is what most meeting exports contain
- Keep the menu bar icon readable on a dark menu bar instead of leaving it black
- Keep your chosen app icon as you chose it: it no longer swaps artwork when the
  system switches between light and dark
